AMBLE EARNINGS DATA — HANDOFF TO THE EARNINGS GENERATOR

Please generate the earnings input for one explicitly synthetic toy-town
scenario: 5,000,000 residents over 50 days, exactly 250,000,000 resident-day
rows. Your earnings will be the authoritative income input to our spending,
pack-opening, item-inventory and home-change simulation. Do not generate a
second independent spending economy. Do not claim these are real agent or
model runs, and do not make one model request per resident or row.

FIRST DELIVERY
Send the schema, generation seed/version, profession allocation and wage/job
rules, plus a complete 50-day sample for 200 residents, before the full export.
Include the list of those 200 resident IDs and the simulation's UTC start date.
Keep healthcare, finance and construction explicitly fictional toy-town jobs.

REQUIRED CSV CONTRACT
UTF-8 CSV or CSV.gz, with this exact required header:
resident_id,day,profession,jobs_completed,credits_earned,synthetic

- resident_id: stable unique string containing only letters, digits, hyphens
  or underscores. Prefer amble-000000001 through amble-005000000.
- day: integer 1 through 50.
- profession: healthcare, finance or construction. Keep each resident's
  assigned profession stable; the current importer rejects role changes.
- jobs_completed: nonnegative integer.
- credits_earned: nonnegative integer credits for that resident on that day.
  Positive earnings require at least one completed job. Zero-credit days must
  still be present. Do not export balances as earnings.
- synthetic: true on every row.

Example:
resident_id,day,profession,jobs_completed,credits_earned,synthetic
amble-000000001,1,construction,2,28,true
amble-000000001,2,construction,0,0,true

Exactly one row per (resident_id, day), including zero-income days. Sort by
resident_id lexicographically, then day numerically, so all 50 days for each
resident are adjacent and increasing.

OPTIONAL ACTUAL PER-JOB WAGES
Add a job_wages column containing a JSON array of integer wages, CSV-escaped
normally. Its length must equal jobs_completed and its sum must equal
credits_earned; use [] for zero jobs. Example CSV field: "[14,14]".
Without this column, we will divide each daily total as evenly as possible
among its declared jobs and explicitly label these per-job wages as an
allocation assumption, rather than your original individual job results.

FULL DELIVERY
Compressed partitions are fine. Keep each resident wholly within one shard,
with globally ordered, non-overlapping resident ranges and an explicit shard
order. Our first importer accepts one CSV/CSV.gz path: ordered shards must be
combined into one logical CSV with one header before import.

Include a manifest with simulation ID, seed, generator version/hash, all
parameters and wage rules, synthetic: true, ordered shard paths, SHA-256
hashes, row counts, unique resident count, and resident/job/earnings counts
by profession and day. Validate all 250,000,000 rows, all 5,000,000 residents,
50 rows per resident, no duplicate resident-day pairs, allowed professions,
nonnegative integer values, and the per-job sums if supplied. Report actual
generation time, file sizes and checks performed. Avoid private conversations,
credentials and Atlas uploads.

WHAT WE WILL DO WITH YOUR EARNINGS
Every resident starts with 12 credits. In the default spending scenario,
everyone buys the same editable 8-credit Daily pack after earning each day:
packs_bought = floor((opening_balance + credits_earned) / 8), with the
remaining credits carried forward. This makes cumulative pack counts increase
with cumulative earnings at any common day. Outcomes remain random, so more
packs do not guarantee more items for every individual.

Each pack has an editable default outcome of 90% empty, 9% one item and 1%
two items. Items feed inventory and home changes. Duplicates remain in
inventory; the first permanent copy can furnish the home, and consumables
are used immediately. There is no resale or return of credits.

Around 500,000,000 openings is the proposed funding target, not permission to
invent money or force purchases. At 8 credits each it costs 4,000,000,000
credits. The 12-credit starting balances supply 60,000,000 of that; actual
earnings and each resident's leftover credits determine the feasible number
of openings. Keep your earnings generation assumptions transparent rather
than silently changing them to hit this target.

Please return the sample and manifest first, then the reproducible generation
code and full earnings export. We will join source rows by resident_id and day,
preserve your source simulation ID and file hash as provenance, and assign
world_id/simulation_id to the resulting spending scenario. Do not add starting
balances to credits_earned: our importer supplies the 12-credit grant once.
